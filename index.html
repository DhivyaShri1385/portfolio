About Me

Who I am & what drives me

I'm a Data Science, AI, and Cybersecurity engineer who builds systems that don't just predict — they explain their reasoning and actively defend themselves. I believe intelligent systems in high-stakes domains must be transparent, interpretable, and secure.

My work spans two converging fields: explainable AI for healthcare (medical imaging, brain tumour diagnosis, XAI methods) and behavioral biometric security (real-time anomaly detection, risk-based authentication, honeypot deception systems).

I've built end-to-end production systems using TensorFlow, PyTorch, Flask, FastAPI, and the complete Python ML ecosystem. I'm equally comfortable designing a neural architecture and implementing an AES-256 encrypted data pipeline with salted hashing.

My flagship projects — NeuroVision AI (explainable brain tumour diagnosis) and UEBA Platform (behavioral biometric authentication with honeypot deception) — represent this dual philosophy: AI that sees clearly and systems that defend intelligently.

UEBA — Behavioral Biometric Auth Platform Featured

Real-Time User and Entity Behavior Analytics — a behavioral biometric authentication system that detects impersonation even with the correct password. Captures 25 signals per login (keystroke rhythm, mouse velocity, device fingerprint), trains a per-user Isolation Forest model, and scores every session in under 2 seconds. Features risk-based MFA with OTP on anomaly, an active honeypot that routes attackers to a fabricated company dashboard, AES-256 encrypted storage, SHA-256 salted hashing, and automated security alert emails. Zero predefined dataset — self-generates training data from real sessions.
