About Me

Who I am & what drives me

I'm a Data Science & AI enthusiast with a deep focus on building systems that don't just predict — they explain their reasoning. I believe AI in high-stakes domains like healthcare must be transparent and interpretable, not just accurate.

My work spans the full stack of machine learning: from exploratory data analysis and model architecture design to production-grade APIs, interactive dashboards, and containerized deployments. I bring together research-grade rigor and engineering discipline.

I've worked with IBM Watson for NLP and intelligent workflows, Power BI for business intelligence, and the complete Python ML ecosystem. I'm equally comfortable in a Jupyter notebook and a Docker container.

My flagship project, NeuroVision AI, represents this philosophy — a full-stack explainable AI platform for brain tumour diagnosis, deployable end-to-end with a React UI and clinical PDF reports.
